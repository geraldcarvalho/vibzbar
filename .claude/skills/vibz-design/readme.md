# VIBZ Design System
VIBZ (Vibz Bar LLC): luxury mobile bar in El Dorado Hills, CA (Sacramento, Folsom, Granite Bay, Lake Tahoe). Clients supply the alcohol; VIBZ brings the styled bar, craft and show. Tagline: "The happier hour experience." Audience: hosts of weddings, milestone birthdays, holiday and corporate events.

**Sources:** six uploaded images (in `uploads/`, copied to `assets/`). No codebase, Figma or font files were provided.

## Content fundamentals
Quiet luxury with a playful, punchy voice. Short lines, specific over adjective-heavy. Lowercase headlines are fine ("pour", "the happier hour"). Second person ("you supply, we bring"). Never discount or urgency language (no "limited time", "% off"). No emoji. Examples: "the moment the night begins." · "Pouring now in El Dorado Hills & beyond" · "Mix · Sip · Unwind".

## Visual foundations
- **Color:** Navy #2d3f5e, Slate #7a8fa8, Lavender #d4d8e8, White #f4f6f9; supporting dusk tones Midnight Indigo, Dusty Blue, Cool Grey (moodboard).
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond headlines (bold, tight tracking, or italic), Hurme Sans (Hurme Geometric Sans, self-hosted in `fonts/`) for supporting text, tracked uppercase eyebrows. Cormorant Garamond (self-hosted in `fonts/`) replaces the originally specified Augustine Serif.
- **Texture:** embossed/letterpress paper (tiled "v" script wave), fluted/ridged surfaces, soft window light and palm shadows. Backgrounds are pale cool paper or deep navy plaster; no gradients.
- **Imagery:** moody, low-contrast, cool-toned; one warm accent (lemon, red layer) allowed. Images carry no baked-in text.
- **Shape:** pill buttons, 16px cards, 8px inputs. Shadows are soft, low and navy-tinted; emboss/deboss inner shadows for tactile elements.
- **Motion:** calm, slow fades (160–320ms, ease-out). Hover: slightly deeper navy; press: no shrink, subtle deboss.
- **Borders:** hairline navy at 14% opacity. Transparency/blur: minimal.
