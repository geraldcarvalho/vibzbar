What we are

Not a bar. An experience that happens to have one.

Anyone can pour. We design the hour. A styled bar that belongs in your space. Real glassware, never plastic. Syrups made from scratch. Garnishes you'll photograph before you drink.

And a surprise in every glass — one detail we don't tell you about until your guests find it. We arrive early. We leave it spotless. You never touch a thing. You choose the bottles. We build the night around them.

How it works

Four steps. That's the whole thing.

  1. 01

    Tell us about the event

  2. 02

    Signature cocktails built around your event. You approve every one.

  3. 03

  4. 04

    We handle the rest

    Setup, service, breakdown. You're a guest at your own party.
